Hidden Fees You Might Not Even Know Exist

\n

Ghanaian banks often charge maintenance fees, ATM withdrawal commissions, and currency conversion costs that go unnoticed. Mobile money platforms can levy a 1‑2 % fee on each transaction, which, when you pay for groceries, transport, and utility bills, quickly erodes your cash flow.

\n
    \n
  • Bank account maintenance: $5‑$10 per month.
    • \
  • ATM cash‑out fee after the free limit: $0.50 per withdrawal.
  • Mobile money transaction fee: 1‑2 % of the amount sent.
\n

Tracking these fees can reveal a hidden leak of several hundred dollars each month.

Build a Simple Yet Effective Budget for Accra

\n

Use the 50/30/20 rule as a starting point, but adapt the percentages to local realities. For a salary of $1,500:

\n
    \n
  • Essential expenses (50 %): rent ($400‑$600), utilities ($80), transport ($100), food ($200).
    • \n
  • Discretionary spending (30 %): entertainment, dining out, shopping ($300).
    • \n
  • Savings & debt repayment (20 %): emergency fund, loan payments ($300).
    • \n
\n

Adjust the categories if you have higher rent or family obligations, but keep a clear ceiling for each.
